## Design Context

### Users
People looking to hire Thiago for independent freelance photography work. They are usually non-technical, arrive with little patience, and want to quickly judge style, taste, and trust before reaching out.

### Brand Personality
Human, artistic, elegant. The site should feel personal and refined, but still direct enough to make hiring feel easy.

### Aesthetic Direction
Editorial photography portfolio with a calm, tactile, gallery-like feel. Light and dark themes are both acceptable, but the overall tone should stay understated, intimate, and visually confident.

### Design Principles
1. Show the work first and let the copy support it
2. Keep the tone warm and personal without sounding casual or messy
3. Make hiring and contact paths obvious without turning the page into a sales funnel
4. Use concise language that helps visitors decide fast
5. Keep every text element elegant, specific, and easy to translate
